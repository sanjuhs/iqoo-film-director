## Prepare the current demo

Use the synthetic media fixtures and the attributed public pose image, without
opening a camera or microphone. Keep **SYNTHETIC DEMO** labels on these inputs.
Do not show private device screens, personal files or a person's likeness
without the corresponding permission. Local model/runtime setup and public
fixture attribution must match the evidence notes.

Recent text plans need roughly 25–53 seconds per five-shot draft; latency and wording vary. Reserve a
full minute; while it works, explain the three phases and the measured local
components. If using a saved generated plan instead, announce that it was
**generated earlier on this phone** and give its recorded time. Do not imply a
saved plan was generated live, or an editable template came from the LLM.

Keep captions editable and review the generated wording before export. Use the
synthetic speech clip for the subtitle demonstration and the three numbered
synthetic scenes for editing. Have a previously exported example ready, clearly
labelled as such, if a fresh export exceeds the presentation slot. Originals
remain available. Keep the app visible while exporting.

## Before replacing the storyboard with a live shoot

These tests are still required; the existing synthetic/media tests do not pass
them. Use one outfit, a fixed portrait phone, a clear floor and five short shots.

1. **AirPods/output:** pair earbuds in Android settings and hear a real cue.
   Bluetooth availability does not prove the cue reached them. Test recording
   audio separately; AirPods microphone input is not established.
2. **Normal permissions:** use Android's camera/microphone prompts. Denial must
   leave manual planning/editing useful. Do not bypass permission UI with ADB.
3. **Live posing:** confirm the camera supports preview, capture and analysis
   together. Observe a real subject, absent/cropped states and uncertainty.
   Preserve scene instructions; a detail shot should not receive full-body cues.
4. **Recording:** ensure a long cue finishes fully before the eight-second pose break and spoken countdown; explicitly start a countdown, make a short consented take,
   stop it and play the saved audio/video. Confirm orientation/duration. Cancel
   and restart a countdown; it must still give the full delay.
5. **Quiet performance:** no cue should overlap recorded speech. STOP ends the
   session. Leaving the app stops camera/microphone, and returning requires a
   fresh creator action; verify saved-take handling after interruption.
6. **Creator review:** check generated shot details, text and timing against the
   actual garment and spoken performance. Record useful and failing suggestions,
   latency, memory and thermals; no retake-reduction claim without that study.
7. **Final hardware:** repeat the complete workflow on the required iQOO. Establish
   NPU with actual runtime/backend and parity evidence if pursued. Demonstrate
   actual supported Office Kit phone-to-laptop transfer before claiming it.

Use modest stationary poses. Do not infer distance or obstacle clearance from
pose landmarks. The creator can ignore a suggestion. Selected original clips
and caption drafts stay local; no private-media upload is needed.

## Recovery paths

| Failure | Continue with | Accurate wording |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Planning exceeds its slot or produces a wrong detail | Reviewed saved AI plan or editable starter | “This was generated earlier,” or “This is the labelled template”; correct the invented detail. |
| Live on-device spoken-brief recognition unavailable | Typed brief | “Clip transcription is tested separately; live speech input is unverified here.” |
| TTS/earbud output unavailable | Written cue | “Local synthesis passed; this audible output route has not.” |
| Pose unavailable or unsupported camera combination | Manual scene instruction | “The creator checks framing; the live pose path is unavailable.” |
| No consented live capture | Synthetic assembly path | “No live capture occurred; these editing clips are synthetic.” |
| Subtitle words/times need correction | Edit draft text/timestamps or use manual caption | “These are local draft subtitles, reviewed before export.” |
| Sparse reference misses a cut | User describes the reference beats | “These are approximate sampled observations, not a full trend analysis.” |
| Fresh export fails | Preserved originals/cut list and labelled earlier result | “This fresh export failed; the shown result was exported earlier.” |
| iQOO/Office Kit unavailable | Nothing Phone preparation evidence | “The required event-hardware integration remains pending.” |

## Optional drone appendix

Keep the phone story complete without a drone. Consented imported drone footage
is an optional establishing view, labelled as manual/vendor capture. It does
not establish live custom control. Neo 2/Fly and Mini 4 Pro/SDK are distinct;
no Neo 2 SDK integration has been demonstrated.

The first Mini 4 Pro probe stays propellers-off and read-only: registration,
exact identity, fresh telemetry and changing preview. No motors, takeoff,
recording, gimbal movement, waypoint upload or aircraft-control action belongs
to that test. Do not rebind equipment or update firmware for a presentation.
Later aircraft actions need separate pilot confirmation, validated bounds and
manual override, plus venue permission where applicable.
