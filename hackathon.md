## Judging strategy

These weights were verified in the prior official-guide research; reconfirm
organizer updates at check-in.

| Dimension | Weight | Evidence to build |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| End product quality | 30% | Repeatable brief-to-reviewed-shoot-pack workflow and useful recovery |
| Novelty/impact | 20% | Detect missing fashion-shot coverage; compare with the creator's usual review |
| Creative phone use | 15% | Essential phone-first planning/capture/review on actual iQOO; HackTracker evidence |
| Technical depth | 15% | Actual DJI integration, tested media analysis and measured latency/resources |
| Office Kit usage | 10% | Real supported phone/laptop transfer during event; HackTracker evidence |
| Presentation | 10% | Clear 3–5 minute demo showing a missed shot, pickup and complete sequence |

Productivity is a reasonable proposed fit because the product improves a
creator workflow; Open Innovation is the alternative if organizers classify it
differently. Drone ownership alone is not novelty or an integration result.

Final demonstration must run on required iQOO hardware. NPU acceleration and
Office Kit remain separate hardware integration gates. No model size or NPU
success is promised. Confirm organizer permission for a drone at the venue;
prepare consented imported footage and a propellers-off preview as clearly
labelled alternatives to a flight demonstration.

Before submission, produce new-concept form copy/document/pitch, complete human
self-reports and attestation, confirm event-code eligibility and obtain the actual
submission receipt. Existing archived documents and releases are provenance,
not evidence that this entry is accepted.
